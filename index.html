Questions, answered

Is MUSICLIB safe?

Yes. MUSICLIB keeps your library local on your device. Only download it from this website so you always get the real app.

Is MUSICLIB free?

You can download and use MUSICLIB without paying for the app itself.

Is every song available?

MUSICLIB's discovery feature gives you access to a very wide catalog. Availability of individual tracks can vary by region and source.

Does it work with my own files?

Yes. MUSICLIB is local first, so your own music stays in your library on your device.

Which devices are supported?

Android is available today. iOS and Windows are coming soon.

I'm a rights holder. What do I do?

See the copyright section on the Policies page and send us a takedown request.
